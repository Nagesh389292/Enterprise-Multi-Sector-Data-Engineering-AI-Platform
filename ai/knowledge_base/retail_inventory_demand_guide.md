# Retail & E-Commerce Supply Chain Analytics Guide

## 1. Overview
The Retail Supply Chain module analyzes point-of-sale (POS) events, inventory levels, and demand forecasting metrics across e-commerce channels.

## 2. Key Retail Supply Chain KPIs
- **Inventory Turnover Ratio (ITR)**: Cost of Goods Sold (COGS) divided by average inventory value.
- **Stockout Rate**: Percentage of SKU requests that could not be fulfilled due to zero inventory.
- **Shrinkage Rate**: Unexplained loss of inventory due to theft, administrative error, or vendor fraud.
- **Demand Forecasting Horizon**: PySpark time-series window functions predicting 7-day and 30-day stock replenishment requirements.
